Perceived Usefulness and Generative AI Adoption Intention: A Longitudinal Study

Jane Smith, John Doe
Journal of Information Systems, 2023
DOI: 10.1234/jis.2023.0456

Abstract
The purpose of this study is to examine how perceived usefulness affects generative AI adoption intention among knowledge workers. Drawing on the Technology Acceptance Model, we investigate whether perceived usefulness predicts adoption intention over time. We conducted a longitudinal survey of 512 professionals across three waves. Our results show that perceived usefulness has a significant positive effect on adoption intention. Perceived ease of use also positively influences perceived usefulness. However, the effect of social influence on adoption intention was not significant.

Keywords: generative AI, technology acceptance, perceived usefulness, adoption intention

1. Introduction
Generative AI tools such as large language models have rapidly entered the workplace. Research Question: Does perceived usefulness affect adoption intention of generative AI among knowledge workers?

2. Methodology
We used a longitudinal panel design and structural equation modeling to test our hypotheses. Data were collected from 512 respondents.

3. Results
Perceived usefulness had a significant positive effect on adoption intention (beta = 0.42, p < 0.001).

4. Discussion
This study contributes to the Technology Acceptance Model literature. A limitation of this study is the reliance on self-reported survey data. Future research should examine organizational-level adoption.
